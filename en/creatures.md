# Creatures

All numbers are from `CRTRAITS.TXT` **[data]**. `Fight Value` and `AI Value` are the two columns the computer player uses to weigh armies; how they are used is covered in the AI chapters. "Map stack" is the size range of a random wandering stack.

> **HotA 1.8.1:** twelve creatures have different stats or prices and eight more differ only in AI Value; see [HotA differences: creatures](../hota/#creatures). The 50 creatures HotA adds are listed under [new creatures](../hota/#new-creatures).

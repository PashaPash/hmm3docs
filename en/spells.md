# Magic

From `SPTRAITS.TXT` **[data]**. For damage spells, damage = `× Power` × spell power + the effect value for the caster's school level. The last column is the per-spell value the AI reads when choosing what to cast.

> **HotA 1.8.1:** Fire Wall, Armageddon, the four Protection spells and two elemental summons have different numbers; see [HotA differences: spells](../hota/#spells).
